Mochi's HousePrivacy Policy

Support

Something not working, or an idea for Mochi? Write to hello@chaunguyen.nl and tell me which iPhone or iPad you use.

Purchases

Bought something on another device? Open the Closet, tap Shop, then Restore Purchases. Purchase Help is there too. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.

For a refund, use Purchase Help in the app or request one from Apple.

Drawing for Mochi

Draw with Apple Pencil or your finger: in Play, or by holding iPhone Duo like a book. Mochi hangs your drawings on its wall; you can take one down from the Scrapbook.

To send a drawing, tap Send to a Friend. Your friend opens the link on a device with Mochi's House and taps Hang It Up. If the link doesn't open from a message, copy it into Safari.

Your Mochi on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Mochi's House. Your Mochi comes along.
