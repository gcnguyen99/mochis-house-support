Privacy Policy

Mochi's House doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your Mochi, its letters and what you tell it stay on your device. If you use iCloud, your game is saved in your own iCloud account so it can sync between your devices; we can't see it.

Step counts are read on your device to find treasures and are never sent anywhere.

If you turn on weather, your approximate location is sent to Apple Weather to get the weather where you are. We never see it, and it isn't stored.

Photos you choose or take for the Photo Booth stay on your device. Only the photo you pick is used, and only when you share the picture does it leave your device.

Treasure hunt photos are kept inside the app on this device. They aren't synced with your game, and removing one from the scrapbook deletes it.

Friend codes only contain how a Mochi looks and a random number. Sharing one is your choice.

Drawings stay on your device. When you send one to a friend, the drawing, your name and your Mochi's friend code travel inside the link you share; nothing goes through a server of ours.

Tug of War and Draw with a Friend run over SharePlay in your FaceTime call. Only your name, how your Mochi looks, and your pulls or the lines you draw are shared with the people in the call.

Reminders and the alarm are scheduled on your device.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.
