Mochi's House

A tiny pet for your pocket. Coming soon to the App Store.

Made for iPhone Duo

Mochi lives in the fold. Every way you hold iPhone Duo is a different place in Mochi's house:

  • Closed: Mochi rides along on the outer display, finds treasures on your walks and gets squished when you fold it shut.
  • Open: the whole room on the big display, with Mochi on one side and care, finds and weather on the other.
  • Half open like a laptop: a little arcade, with the game above the fold and the buttons on the half lying on the table.
  • Like a laptop at bedtime: Mochi's nightstand, with sleep sounds, an alarm and Mochi dozing off.
  • Held like a book: Mochi's storybook with a new letter every morning, and a sketchbook to draw for Mochi with Apple Pencil or your finger.
  • Standing as a tent: Mochi falls asleep on the outer display.
  • Flat on the table: Tug of War or Doodle Guess with the person across from you, each on their own half.

Also on iPhone and iPad

Feed, bathe and pat Mochi, walk together for treasures and outfits, read a letter from Mochi every morning, draw for Mochi, play with friends over SharePlay, and keep Mochi on your Home Screen. Mochi never gets sick and never punishes you for a missed day.
